Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

The short version: we only ever collect what we need to quote and carry out your repair. Your details go straight to us — we don't sell them, share them with advertisers, or use them for marketing.

What we collect

  • If you send us a quote request: your name, phone number or email address, details of your vehicle, a description of the damage, and any photos you choose to upload.
  • If you accept the privacy note on first visit: a tiny anonymous marker that lets us count how many different people visit the site. It doesn't contain your name or any personal details.

What we use it for

  • To answer your quote request and get in touch about the job.
  • To keep a record of the work we've done for you.
  • To see roughly how many people find the website, so we know it's being found.

Where your information goes

Your quote request lands directly with us by email, and a copy is kept in a private online folder we use for our business records. It is not passed to other companies, marketing lists, or advertising networks. We are a small local business — there is no call centre and no data broker.

Cookies and similar items

We don't use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or analytics services like Google Analytics. The only things we store in your browser are:

  • Your Accept / No-thanks choice from the privacy note (so we don't ask twice).
  • The anonymous visit marker described above, and only if you accept.

If you clear your browser data, we'll simply ask you again next time.

How long we keep it

We keep quote records and job notes for as long as it's sensible for a trades business (warranty queries, recalls, disputes). If you'd rather we deleted yours at any point, just ask — we'll remove it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct anything that's wrong, or delete it. No forms or legal language needed — a quick phone call or email is enough.
